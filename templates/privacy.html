Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address and password. As you use Spendly we also store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run the service: to sign you in, to store and display your expenses, and to produce summaries and filters for you. We do not sell your personal information or use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on servers we control and protected with reasonable security measures, including hashed passwords. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. We do not share your expense data with any third party. If this changes, we will update this policy and tell you.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data deleted, contact us at support@spendly.example.
